Common questions

How do I get an office?

Tap Request an Office on the Board and pick your days. If an office is free, it's assigned to you right away. If not, you join the waitlist and get one automatically when someone offers theirs on those days.

How do I offer my office?

Tap Offer My Office, choose your office and the days you'll be away. The next person on the waitlist gets it automatically. You keep your office on every other day.

Why am I still on the waitlist when I can see an open office?

An office is only assigned for the exact days it's offered. Tap your name on the Waitlist tab to see which days you still need. If offices are open on those days, people who asked before you were placed in them first.

How do I cancel?

Open the Plans tab and swipe left on the request or offer. Any office it frees up goes to the next person waiting.

I lost my organization's access code.

Ask the person who registered your organization. If you registered it and lost the code, email us.

How do I delete my information?

Cancel your requests and offers in the Plans tab. To have your organization's shared data deleted, email us. See the Privacy Policy for details.
