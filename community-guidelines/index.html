Community Guidelines

OfficeSwap works because people trust each other with their offices. These guidelines keep it that way for everyone in your organization, whether you're offering your office or borrowing someone else's.

V1Last updated: October 2026 · 11 sections

How we share

Treat every office like it's yours.

Book only the days you'll really use.

Leave it ready for the next person.

Everyone who uses OfficeSwap agrees to these Community Guidelines, along with our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Breaking them can mean losing access to your organization's schedule. Details on how that works are below.

01Leave it how you found it

Treat someone else's office the way you'd want yours treated. Clear the desk when you go, take your things home, and throw your rubbish away.

Don't rearrange the furniture, unplug the owner's equipment or adjust things you can't put back. If something breaks or goes missing while you're there, tell the owner (or your office manager) the same day.

02Respect what's personal

An office you borrow still belongs to someone. Don't open drawers or cabinets, read papers left out, use personal items, or log in to anything that isn't yours.

If you see something confidential lying around, leave it where it is.

03Only book what you'll use

Request an office only for days you'll really be in. Holding one "just in case" keeps it from the next person on the waitlist.

Plans change: cancel a request in Plans as soon as you know, so the office goes straight to someone who needs it. Don't make extra requests under other names to get ahead in line. First come, first served only works if everyone plays fair.

04Offer what's really free

Offer only your own office, and only for days you'll really be away.

If you come back early and need to cancel an offer, remember someone may already be counting on that office. Let them know as soon as you can.

05Use your real name

Request and offer offices under your own name, the one your coworkers know you by. Don't request offices for someone else without asking them first.

Everyone in your organization can see the names on the board, so keep them accurate and professional.

06Keep notes helpful

Notes are for whoever uses your office next: where the monitor cable is, how the blinds work, which drawer is free to use.

Everyone in your organization can read them, so never put passwords, door codes or other private information in a note, and keep the tone friendly and work-appropriate.

07Share photos with care

Only add photos you have the right to use. Add a photo of a person only with their permission, and never upload anything offensive, misleading or unrelated to work.

Photos are shared with everyone in your organization.

08Keep your access code inside the team

Your organization's access code lets anyone who has it see and change your schedule. Share it only with people in your organization, and never post it publicly.

If it gets out, tell whoever set up OfficeSwap for your organization and email us at support@officeswap.co.

09Your workplace rules still apply

OfficeSwap helps you share offices; it doesn't replace your employer's policies on security, safety, visitors or conduct. When they differ, follow your workplace's rules.

10Zero tolerance for harassment

No harassment, threats, discrimination or hateful content anywhere in OfficeSwap: not in names, notes, photos or office details. This includes jokes at someone's expense.

11Reporting and what happens next

See something that breaks these guidelines? Tell your office manager, or email support@officeswap.co. We read every report.

Whoever holds your organization's master code can remove offices or people from it. We may remove content that breaks these guidelines and, in serious or repeated cases, suspend an organization's access, as described in our Terms of Service.

Questions about these guidelines? Email support@officeswap.co.
