Getting started

What is OfficeSwap?

An iPhone app for workplaces where some people have their own office and others don't. Owners offer their office on days they're away, people without an office request one, and the app matches them automatically.

How do I join my organization?

Open the app and enter your organization's access code. Ask whoever set up OfficeSwap for your team if you don't have it. There's no account, email or password.

How do I set up OfficeSwap for a new organization?

Tap Register a new organization on the first screen. Enter the organization's name and office address to get an access code. Then add your offices and who owns each one, and share the code with your team.

Can I change the office list later?

Yes. Open the Settings tab and tap Edit office list. Put one office per line: office name, owner, location, address. Leave the owner blank for a guest desk.

Is there an Android version?

Not at the moment. OfficeSwap is for iPhone.

Requesting an office

How do I get an office?

On the Board, tap Request an Office, choose your name and pick the days you'll be in. If an office is free on those days, it's assigned to you right away.

What happens if nothing is free?

You join the waitlist. As soon as someone offers an office on one of your days, it's assigned to you automatically. You don't need to check back.

Why am I on the waitlist when I can see an open office?

Offices are only assigned for the exact days they're offered. Tap your name on the Waitlist tab to see which days you still need. If offices are open on those days, people who asked before you were placed in them first.

Who gets an office first?

First come, first served. The earliest request always gets the next open office.

I need several days. Will I have to move offices every day?

OfficeSwap keeps you in as few different offices as possible, so you'll often have the same office for your whole stay.

Can my office be taken away after I'm assigned it?

Your assignment stays put unless the owner takes back their offer. If that happens, you go back on the waitlist and get the next office that opens up.

Why isn't my name in the list?

People who already have a request for today or a later day aren't listed, so nobody gets a duplicate. To change your days, cancel your request in the Plans tab and make a new one with all the days you need.

Offering your office

How do I offer my office?

On the Board, tap Offer My Office, choose your office and pick the days you'll be away. You can add a note for whoever uses it, like where the monitor cable is.

Do I lose my office?

No. It's only shared on the days you pick. You keep it on every other day.

Why isn't my office in the list?

Offices that already have an offer for today or a later day aren't listed. To change your days, cancel your offer in the Plans tab and offer it again.

What's a guest desk?

A desk or office with no owner. It's always available and can be assigned to anyone who requests an office.

Changes and cancellations

How do I cancel a request or an offer?

Open the Plans tab and swipe left on it. Any office it frees up goes to the next person waiting.

Does everyone see the same schedule?

Yes. Everyone in your organization shares one schedule. Your changes are sent right away, and the app checks for other people's changes every 30 seconds and whenever you open it.

What does Reset data do?

It's in the Settings tab. It clears your organization's schedule and starts over, for everyone, not just on your phone. Use it carefully.

Data and privacy

What information does OfficeSwap store?

Only what your team enters: names, offices, office addresses, the days people request or offer, and any notes. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Who can see our schedule?

Anyone with your organization's access code can see and change it in the app. The schedule is also kept in a Google Sheet that anyone with its link can view. Keep both the code and the link private.

Can I get the data in a spreadsheet?

Yes. The Export tab opens the live Google Sheet, or lets you share a CSV file for Excel or Numbers.

How do I delete our data?
