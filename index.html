How it works

Three steps, and nobody has to coordinate anything by email.

Owners offer their office

Going to be away? Pick the days in Offer My Office and add a note if you like. You keep your office on every other day.

Others request one

Need a place to work? Pick your days in Request an Office. If one is free, it's yours right away.

OfficeSwap matches them

Nothing free yet? You join the waitlist and get an office the moment one opens up on your days. No need to check back.

Everything in one place

Everyone in your organization sees the same up-to-date schedule on their own phone.

Fair, automatic, and simple

Built for teams who share a floor, not for IT departments.

First come, first served

The earliest request always gets the next open office.

Fewer moves

If you need several days, OfficeSwap keeps you in as few different offices as possible.

No accounts

No sign-ups and no passwords. Everyone joins with your organization's access code.

Set up in minutes

Register your organization with its name and address, then paste in your offices and who owns each one.

Guest desks

Desks with no owner are always open to be assigned.

Live spreadsheet

The schedule is kept in a Google Sheet anyone with the link can view, and exports to CSV for Excel or Numbers.

Several locations

Filter the board by location if your organization has more than one.

No ads, no tracking

OfficeSwap only stores the schedule your team enters. Read the Privacy Policy.
