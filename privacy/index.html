Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

The short version

  • OfficeSwap has no user accounts. It doesn't ask for your email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • It stores the office schedule your organization enters: people's names, offices, office addresses, dates and optional notes.
  • That schedule is kept in a Google Sheet so everyone in your organization sees the same data. Anyone who has the sheet's link can view it.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell your information.

This policy explains how the OfficeSwap iPhone app ("OfficeSwap", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information the app handles

OfficeSwap only handles information that people in your organization type into it:

  • Organization details: the organization's name, its office address, and its access code.
  • People's names: the names of office owners and of people who request offices.
  • Office details: each office's name, owner, location and address.
  • Schedule details: the days someone requests an office, the days an owner offers their office, which office each person is assigned, and any note an owner adds when offering their office.

The app does not collect your email address, phone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, health information, payment information, device advertising identifier, or browsing history. It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and it shows no ads.

Please don't enter sensitive personal information, such as health details or the reason for an absence, in notes or names.

How the information is used

The information is used only to run the app:

  • to show which offices are open, in use, or occupied by their owner on each day;
  • to match office requests to offered offices automatically;
  • to keep a waitlist of requests that haven't been matched yet;
  • to keep a readable copy of the schedule in your organization's Google Sheet.

We don't use it for advertising or profiling, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Where the information is stored

On your iPhone

The app keeps a copy of your organization's schedule on your device. It also keeps the organizations you have signed in to, their access codes and addresses, and a spreadsheet (CSV) file of the schedule that you can choose to share. This data stays on your device unless you share it. Deleting the app removes it.

In Google Sheets

So that everyone in an organization sees the same schedule, the app saves the schedule to a Google Sheet held in a Google account controlled by the app's developer. The app connects to Google Sheets and Google Drive automatically. You don't sign in to Google, and the app never sees your own Google account.

The sheet is set so that anyone with its link can view it. The app shows the link in its Export tab. Anyone you give the link to, or who otherwise gets it, can see the names, offices, addresses, dates and notes it contains.

Google processes this data under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Who can see the information

  • People in your organization: anyone who opens the app with your organization's access code sees its full schedule, including names and notes.
  • Anyone with the sheet's link: as described above.
  • Service providers: Google stores the shared schedule. Apple distributes the app under its own terms.
  • When required by law: we may disclose information if we must do so to comply with the law or a valid legal request, or to protect the rights and safety of people using the app.

We don't share the information with anyone else.

How long information is kept

Information is kept until it's removed. People can cancel their own requests and offers in the app. The app's Reset data option clears and replaces an organization's shared schedule for everyone. Past entries may stay in the shared schedule until they're removed or reset. To ask us to delete an organization's shared data, contact us as described below.

Security

Data travels between the app and Google over encrypted connections. An access code is a simple way to open an organization's schedule, not a password, and anyone who knows it can see that schedule. Keep your organization's access code and sheet link private. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee the security of information you enter.

Your choices and rights

You can view, change or cancel your own requests and offers in the app at any time. You can also contact us to ask for a copy of information about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it. Depending on where you live, such as California or the European Economic Area, you may have additional rights under local law. We will respond to requests as those laws require.

Children

OfficeSwap is a workplace tool and isn't intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child's information has been entered, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app or its App Store listing. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
